Privacy Policy — Pizza Bench

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Pizza Bench is made by one person. This policy is short because the app collects almost nothing, and everything below is a description of what the code actually does — not a template.

Your recipes, your bakes, your photos and your notes never leave your phone. There is no account, no login, and no cloud copy of anything you make.

Three things do leave your phone: a small set of anonymous usage events, and — only if you type them in yourself — your email address and any feedback you write.

What stays on your device, always

  • Every recipe and formula you create or edit
  • Every bake you log, including the timings you tap
  • Every photo you take. Photos are never uploaded anywhere
  • Your kitchen profile — your ovens, their temperatures, your unit preferences
  • Your evaluations, findings and version history

This information is stored only in the app's own storage on your phone. Deleting the app deletes all of it permanently. We cannot see it, recover it, or restore it for you.

What is sent

Anonymous usage events

Nine events are sent so I can see where people get stuck:

app_open onboarding_completed bake_started bake_completed evaluation_saved diagnosis_viewed pro_viewed pro_clicked pro_email_submitted

Each carries an anonymous install identifier — 16 random characters generated on your device. It is not your name, your account or your device ID, it is not shared with anyone, and reinstalling the app generates a new one. It exists only so that twenty events from one person are not counted as twenty people.

Each also carries which version of the app you are running, plus small non-personal details of the event itself — for example the oven temperature you chose, or whether a bake was rated a keeper.

What is deliberately not sent: the content of your recipes, your photos, your notes, your bake history, and detailed records of how you move around the app.

Your email address — only if you give it

Two places in the app ask for an email, and both are optional:

  • The Pro screen, if you want to be told when it is ready
  • The feedback form, if you want a reply

It is used for that and nothing else. It is not added to a mailing list, not sold, not shared, and not used for advertising.

Feedback you write

If you use the feedback form, what you write is sent along with the screen you were on. That is the point of the form.

Where it goes

To a private Google Sheet that only I can access. There is no analytics company involved, no advertising network, and no third-party tracking SDK anywhere in this app.

What is never collected

No location. No contacts. No device identifiers for advertising. No browsing or cross-app activity. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone, for any purpose.

Camera and photo permissions

The app asks for camera and photo library access so you can attach photos to a bake. Those photos are saved on your phone and go nowhere else. You can refuse the permission and the app still works — you simply won't have photos to compare.

Notifications

Notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device — nothing is sent to a server to make them appear. They tell you when a stage of your bake is done.

Deleting your data

On your phone: delete the app. Everything the app stored locally goes with it.

Anything you sent: email tomerkoresh61@gmail.com and ask, or use the feedback form in the app. I will delete your email address and any feedback you sent. Since usage events are anonymous, they cannot be traced back to you — but if you tell me your install identifier I will remove those rows too.

Children

Pizza Bench is not directed at children and is not designed for them.

If this policy changes

The date at the top changes with it. If a change ever means collecting something new, it will be requested in the app first — it will not happen quietly.
